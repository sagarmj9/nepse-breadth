How to read it

  • 20 DMA reacts first (early signal); 50 DMA confirms the bigger trend.
  • When most stocks are above their DMAs (green), the market is healthy. When most are below (red), it is weak.
  • Extremes matter: too many solid greens in a row means the market is stretched and may pause; long red streaks can mean it is washed out and close to a bounce.
  • Up/Down 4.5% today and 20% in 5 days show how strong buying or selling pressure is.
  • 52W net = new 52-week highs minus new 52-week lows. Positive and rising is healthy.
  • Before 2026 fewer stocks were counted (some merged companies are missing from old data), so compare old years with the % columns. NEPSE index values start from Nov 2025.
