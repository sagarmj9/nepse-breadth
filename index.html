How to read it

  • New highs vs new lows: more stocks making new highs than new lows (positive net) means buyers are in control. The 1-month row reacts first; 6-month and 52-week confirm a real trend.
  • 20 DMA reacts first (early signal); 50 DMA confirms the bigger trend. Green = most stocks above (strong), red = most below (weak).
  • Monthly thrust above zero means more stocks have jumped 25%+ in a month than crashed 25%+: strong momentum.
  • Extremes matter: too many solid greens in a row means the market is stretched and may pause; long red streaks can mean it is washed out and close to a bounce.
  • Before 2026 fewer stocks were counted (some merged companies are missing from old data), so compare old years with the % columns. NEPSE index values start from Nov 2025.
